Adventures in Fire Island

This adventure was interesting and peaceful.

Trip Highlights

My recent trip to Fire Island National Seashore was an unforgettable coastal getaway. From walking along the quiet, sun-drenched beaches to climbing the historic Fire Island Lighthouse for panoramic ocean views, every moment felt peaceful and refreshing. Exploring the Sunken Forest boardwalk and spotting local wildlife along the dunes were definitely the highlights of the adventure.
